# Cameo

> Cameo is a consumer marketplace for personalized video messages from celebrities, athletes, musicians, comedians, and creators. Fans book a custom shout-out by sending a short script; the talent records and delivers a bespoke video, typically within seven days. Cameo also runs Cameo for Business, gift cards, and the Red Carpet Rewards loyalty program. Cameo does not publish a public developer API or OpenAPI — this file describes the company's public web, help, and security surface.

## Company
- [Cameo](https://www.cameo.com): Book personalized video messages from 50,000+ celebrities and creators.
- [Cameo for Business](https://www.cameo.com/c/business): Branded talent video content for businesses.
- [Cameo Blog](https://www.cameo.com/blog): Company news and talent stories.
